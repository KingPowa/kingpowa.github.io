## Context

The **Apple Developer Academy** is an intensive, project-based program where learning happens primarily through **building real products**.  
Coming from a non-traditional background, I started with limited app development experience and gradually learned how to:

- Translate ideas into working apps  
- Design for real users, not just features  
- Iterate quickly based on feedback  
- Work effectively in multidisciplinary teams
